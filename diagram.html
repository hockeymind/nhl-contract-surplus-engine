Contract Surplus Engine

For every contract, the model asks one question: how many dollars of on-ice value does the player deliver over the term, compared with the cap hit? It values the player in goals above replacement, forecasts him forward, prices that forecast against what the market actually paid, then adds up the value year by year against a salary cap that grows.

The model in one picture

What each stage does

Stage 1

Player value: goals above replacement

  • Six components: even-strength offence, even-strength defence, power-play offence, penalty-kill defence, penalties, finishing.
  • Each rate is measured against the player's own team, so a good team does not inflate every skater.
  • Rates are shrunk toward the position average. Weight on the player's own record = τ² / (τ² + k/hours); τ² (true talent spread) and k (noise) come from year-over-year covariance, by position group.
  • A penalty is worth 0.178 goals. Finishing is multiplied by 0.89, the slope of next-season finishing on this season's shrunk value.
  • Replacement level: forwards ranked 13th or lower and defensemen ranked 7th or lower on their team's ice time. WAR = GAR ÷ 6.
Stage 2

Forecast

f = −1.93 + 0.903·proj82 + 0.407·g1 + 0.085·g2 − 0.778·hist1
  • proj82 is last full season GAR/82 plus the age adjustment; g1, g2 are the earlier seasons; hist1 is longer history.
  • Each contract is forecast from the last full season completed before the signing date.
  • Realized seasons are rescaled to the info season's spread: the spread of GAR grew 15–25% after 2020.
Stage 3

Price model

Forwards: 0.16 + 0.335·f + 0.07·UFA + 0.05·(age−27) + 1.91·ln(term)
Defense: 1.45 + 0.257·f − 0.15·UFA + 0.08·(age−27) + 2.40·ln(term)
  • Output is cap share in %. Term is the biggest driver: adding ln(term) lifts R² from 0.58 to 0.73 (forwards) and 0.31 to 0.61 (defense); fitted on all 2018–26 signings.
  • Separate curves because defensemen are paid like forwards at much lower GAR; one curve grades nearly every defenseman as overpaid.
  • Residual = actual cap share − fitted. Positive means paid above what the forecast implies.
Stage 4

Market adjustment and year-1 surplus

f* = −0.10 + 1.01·f + 1.10·resid
surplus₁ = k·(f* − f) − resid
  • The price carries skill the model cannot see, so a price premium raises the forecast. Only k × 1.10 ≈ 37% (forwards) and 28% (defense) of a premium comes back as performance.
  • Adjusting cuts forecast error on the 2023–24 test deals from 7.05 to 6.74.
  • Year-1 value in % of cap = cap share + surplus₁.
Stage 5

Contract path

f*_t = f*₁ + 0.53 · Σ age deltas
value_t = value₁ + k·(f*_t − f*₁), floor 0.8% of cap
surplus_t = value_t × cap_t − cap hit
  • The age curve, applied unscaled, was too pessimistic for signed players. The fitted scale is 0.53 (SE 0.10).
  • The cap hit is flat in dollars while the cap grows, so a long deal gets cheaper relative to the cap each year.
Uncertainty

The 80% band

Var(total) = 0.93² × [ 3.03·(N·sd82)² + 4.01·N(N+1)(2N+1)/6 + 24.33·N ]
  • N is the term in years, sd82 the player's own estimate uncertainty per 82 games. Three parts: today's uncertainty about the player carried through every year, drift that grows with horizon, and season-to-season noise.
  • Converted to dollars through k and the cap, then total ± 1.28 SD gives the 80% band. The band is fitted at 4% cap growth.

One real contract, year by year

Across 71 eight-year deals the average projected surplus is −$7.4M with a flat cap, +$1.7M at 2% growth, +$2.9M at 4% and +$4.2M at 6%. Cap growth, not aging, moves long-deal grades the most, and growth after 2026-27 is an assumption.

What the model does not do

Known bias

  • Forwards at 7% of the cap or more have beaten their forecasts, by about 1 GAR/82 on average over 2018–23 (SE 0.6) but by 2.3 for 2021+ signings, against −0.8 for 2018–20. The workbook applies the +1.0 average, so surplus for big forwards is probably still understated.
  • Defense grades are provisional: GAR undercounts defensive value. Workbook leaderboards exclude defensemen by default.
  • The penalty-kill defence component has a calibration slope of 1.31 that is not yet corrected, and its standard error is not computed.

Not covered

  • Goalies, injuries and availability, and players with no NHL history.
  • Cap hit stands in for salary actually paid; contracts under $1M are excluded.
  • Backtests drop players under 40 games since signing, which flatters long-horizon results.
  • Contracts signed 2025 and later have at most one realized season, so they are priced and projected but not used to fit the forecast. The workbook has a repricing input for the 2026 jump in top-end prices.
